# Black Boxes

This file will record products that remain unnecessarily difficult to understand, repair, or reuse.

Especially notable are **abandoned black boxes**: products that are no longer sold or supported, while useful technical information remains closed.

Possible evidence:

- unavailable schematics or service information;
- undocumented proprietary interfaces that block repair/reuse;
- discontinued products with no technical release;
- avoidable barriers to replacement or diagnosis;
- community reconstruction required to recover basic technical knowledge.

This is a living technical record, not a permanent sentence.

If a product becomes more open, its status should change and the history should remain visible.

_No entries yet. The list starts with evidence, not reputation._
