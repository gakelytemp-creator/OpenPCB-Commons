# Open Boxes

This file will record products and manufacturers that make understanding, repair, reuse, and long-term technical life easier.

Possible reasons for inclusion:

- schematics published;
- service documentation available;
- diagnostic interfaces documented;
- firmware interfaces documented;
- mechanical CAD or part information available;
- discontinued products opened after commercial life;
- repair parts and procedures maintained;
- second-life reuse supported.

Status can change over time.

A manufacturer that opens a formerly closed product should be moved accordingly and the history preserved.
