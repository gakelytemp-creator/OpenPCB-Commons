# Circuit Patterns

OpenPCB Commons should collect not only complete reconstructed boards, but also **reusable schematic ideas**.

A circuit often contains a small structure that appears again and again across unrelated products: a protection trick, bias network, current source, level shifter, snubber, startup circuit, sensing arrangement, gate driver, oscillator fragment, feedback compensation method, or power-stage pattern.

This folder is intended to classify and compare such structures.

A useful record may include:

- pattern name and aliases;
- functional purpose;
- minimal schematic;
- common variants;
- operating principle;
- assumptions and limits;
- typical failure modes;
- measurement clues;
- examples found on real boards;
- related parts that can substitute;
- links to educational explanations in the preparatory course;
- provenance and evidence.

The goal is to move from:

"I saw this circuit once."

to:

**"I recognize what family of idea this belongs to, why it is used, and where else it can live."**

Patterns should remain open to correction and alternative interpretation.

Over time, Noepedia can make these relations searchable across boards, functions, measurements, repairs, and second-life uses.
